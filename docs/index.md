# D&D Beyond Gamelog – test builds

D&D Beyond Gamelog (module id `ddb-game-log`) brings your D&D Beyond game log into Foundry VTT. Rolls made on D&D Beyond show up as chat cards in your Foundry world.

This site is for testers of **version 3**. You are a Foundry GM or a player in a GM's world; you do not need to know anything about code.

{: .warning }
You are looking at a test build. It may break. Please report problems, see [Feedback]({{ '/feedback.html' | relative_url }}).

## Current test phase

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Phase | Closed alpha |
| Current test version | 3.0.0-alpha.5 |
| Foundry VTT | v13 minimum, v14 verified |
| Game system | dnd5e 5.0.0 or newer |

The test runs in three phases. All dates are tentative.

| Phase | Dates | Who can join | Access | You get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Closed alpha | October 12–25, 2026 | Everyone who has Gamelog MAX now or had it at some point | 7 days | Gamelog MAX features |
| Closed beta | October 26–November 8, 2026 | Everyone who has Gamelog PRO or MAX now, plus everyone who tested the closed alpha | 7 days | Your own tier, at least PRO |
| Open beta | November 9–15, 2026 | Anyone, no Patreon membership needed | 3 days | Gamelog PRO features |

You join from inside Foundry: on the **Connection** page of Gamelog Config, click **Join the test**, see [First steps]({{ '/first-steps.html' | relative_url }}#join-the-test). In the two closed phases, link Patreon first so the server can check whether you can join.

## How to join

1. Make sure you meet the [requirements]({{ '/install.html' | relative_url }}#requirements).
2. [Install the test build]({{ '/install.html' | relative_url }}) in Foundry.
3. Follow [First steps]({{ '/first-steps.html' | relative_url }}) to connect your world and press **Join the test**.
4. Roll something on D&D Beyond and look at your Foundry chat.
5. Tell us how it went in [Feedback]({{ '/feedback.html' | relative_url }}).
